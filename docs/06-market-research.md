# Market Research — condensed from 4 deep-research reports (27 Jul 2026, ~70 sources)

## 1. The market gap (why we win)
- **No direct competitor exists**: nobody combines voice AI × dementia-specific engagement × Indian languages × family dashboard. Closest: **Anvayaa** (human dementia visits 3×/week — proves families pay, at human cost), **LifeBio** (English, B2B nursing homes, RCT-validated reminiscence app), **inTouch** (€29.90/mo daily AI calls, English), **Meela** ($40/mo, loneliness-general). India-side (Emoha ₹400–12k/mo, Khyaal ₹999/yr, Samarth, GenWise, Ivory) — none do dementia engagement.
- **Price anchors**: trained dementia attendant ₹40,000+/mo; Bangalore memory day-care ₹25–85k/mo; Goodfellows human companionship ₹5,000/mo; Samvedna sells CST at ₹1,000–5,000 **per session**.
- **Positioning line**: *"India's first AI voice companion for dementia — daily reminiscence therapy in her own language for ₹50 a day, when the alternative is a ₹40,000-a-month attendant who doesn't speak it."*

## 2. Caregiver pain (what we honestly solve)
- Chennai (SCARF) study: caregivers **explicitly ask for daily engagement help** — "simple activities or games." Today: TV.
- The AI absorbs **repetitive questioning** (the #1 documented burden driver) without fatigue.
- **Personhood**: elders describe being treated "like a kid" — being asked about your life in your language counters identity erasure. Chennai reminiscence trial: +12% well-being, +16% QoL.
- **Guilt-free**: caregivers crave respite but refuse outside help ("log kya kahenge"). A voice companion isn't an outsider in the home — *"care that stays in the family."*
- **We do NOT claim**: wandering, night care, ADL help, agitation reduction, financial relief.
- **90% of Indian dementia is undiagnosed** (median urban diagnosis delay: 24 months; half of families call it normal aging). → Consumer face says "memory companion for aging parents", never leads with dementia. Recall-difficulty tracking quietly surfaces the diagnosis conversation — we create our own diagnosed market.

## 4. Evidence base (citations for the deck/site)
- **I-CONECT RCT** (Gerontologist 2024): structured photo-prompted conversation 4×/week → cognition **d=0.73** in MCI. *This is a human-run Yaadein. Lead with it.*
- **CST Cochrane 2023** (CD005562, 37 RCTs): moderate-certainty cognition benefit, best at ≥2×/week. Protocol themes map to voice: proverbs (canonical iCST "Old Wives' Tales" activity), category naming, food, current-affairs opinions, songs. **CST-India (SCARF/Chennai, Tamil-validated)** adaptations: film songs, rangoli/kolam, festival foods, proverbs.
- **CSTalk 2025**: CST already prototyped on a voice assistant (Alexa+GPT-4o) — feasible & accepted.
- **Home iCST RCT (n=356) was NULL** — because family delivery collapsed. Our framing: *the AI removes the adherence failure that sank home CST.* Never claim proven solo-home cognition gains.
- **Music**: favorite-song memory survives late into Alzheimer's (Brain 2015); Cochrane: helps depression/mood, NOT agitation. Antakshari = culturally native retrieval practice. She sings; TTS can't.
- **Digital biomarker citations** (for recall-latency tracking): silent-pause analysis tracks decline (Alz Assoc 2025); dementia detectable from voice-assistant interaction (Comp Speech & Lang 2021); longitudinal speech biomarkers review (2025). **Category naming = semantic verbal fluency = classic dementia screen** → each game round silently administers a validated instrument. *"The game is the therapy AND the biomarker."*
- **Red lines**: never quiz day/date (frustration/agitation beyond early stage — orientation as statements only); games errorless & opinion-first; no "brain training" claims.
