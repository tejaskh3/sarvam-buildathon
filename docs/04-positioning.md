# Yaadein — Positioning & Competitive Landscape

*(Researched July 2026)*

## The landscape — we are not alone, and we shouldn't claim to be

| Player | What they do | Where they fall short of Yaadein |
|---|---|---|
| **LifeBio Memory** (US) | Closest incumbent — AI life-story app, voice prompts, sold into senior living | English-first, US market. Interview-style capture producing a document, not a longitudinal relationship |
| **KindredMind** (US) | Closest in spirit — answers her phone calls, personal knowledge base, validation therapy | Knowledge base is **uploaded by the caregiver, static**. Consumes family knowledge; doesn't generate new knowledge from her |
| **Sunny** (US) | Conversation practice for MCI, research-backed | Conversation as exercise — nothing persists, no family artifact |

| **Ivory** 🇮🇳 | Brain-health exercises | Cognitive training — the quiz model we explicitly reject |

**The honest sentence:** the category exists; our specific object in it does not. In India, nobody does reminiscence at all. Globally, the reminiscence players are English-first and their "memory" is a static family-uploaded profile.

## What exactly didn't happen before Yaadein

1. **Her stories died in the room.** No mechanism existed by which an elder's ordinary daily speech accumulated into anything — least of all in Marathi-Hindi-English code-mix. After: every session deposits provenance-graded, audio-anchored memories that compound.
2. **Personalized reminiscence was rationed by human prep time.** ~35 min prep/resident/session → 6 of 40 residents. The other 34 sessions *literally did not occur*. After: prep is zero, so the session exists at all.
3. **The family flew blind into every visit.** No product produces the pre-visit briefing. KindredMind moves information family→agent; **we are the only one moving it agent→family.**
4. **Nobody kept honest books on a dementia patient's memory.** Every existing system stores what she says as flat fact. Provenance grades, variance versioning, family arbitration, retrieval-enforced topic policy have no equivalent — we're the first to treat "she said 2, then 3, then 4" as data to resolve rather than a value to overwrite.

## The layered claim (never lean on "regional" alone)

"We're the regional version" invites *"so it's LifeBio plus a translation API?"* — an L2 Creativity score. Layer it:

1. **Access:** "This category exists in English. For 60 crore Indians whose mother is remembering her life in Marathi or Tamil, it exists nowhere."
2. **Why regional ≠ translation:** "Her speech is code-mixed mid-sentence, her pauses are longer, her wedding invitation is in Devanagari, her grandchildren read English. The language problem is a *pipeline* problem — that's why we're Sarvam end to end."
3. **New even vs. the English players:** "Nobody builds her biography *from* conversation instead of consuming a family upload; nobody keeps honest books — versioned answers, family arbitration; nobody sends knowledge *toward* the family as the Sunday briefing."

If layer 1 gets shot down ("Silver Saathi is Hindi"), retreat to layer 3 — it's in the demo, so it's unassailable.

**One-sentence version:** "There are players — none regional, and even the English ones only listen to the family. We're the first that listens to *her*."

## Judge attacks & answers

- **"LifeBio already sells reminiscence AI to care homes."** Yes — in English, interview-style, producing a document. Concede, then pivot to direction-of-flow and honest-books, which are checkable in our demo.
- **"What stops Silver Saathi from adding this?"** The moat isn't the voice pipeline — anyone can call Sarvam. It's the memory discipline (provenance, variance, arbitration, policy): unglamorous, safety-critical, and exactly what a reminders company has no reason to build.
- **"Does this slow dementia?"** We don't claim it. The literature says regular engagement *may* help symptoms (Cochrane: small effects, stronger in milder dementia; connection > testing per Alzheimer's Association). We prove engagement, coverage and family connection — on stage, not in a citation.

## Official problem statement alignment

~85% aligned before the PS was released. The PS validated: agent-led conversation, no-testing stance, caregiver uploads, emotional-response tracking, continuity. The PS corrected us: **photos on screen** (we were voice-only → now voice-first), **revisit scheduler**, **recall trajectory tracking** (implemented passively via provenance-grade trends).

Everything in the PS is now table stakes — every team has the same document. Our value lives in what the PS *doesn't* say: Session Contract, provenance/variance/arbitration, Sunday briefing, retrieval-enforced policy, multi-resident isolation, C6 truthfulness rule.
